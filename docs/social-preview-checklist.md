# Social Preview Checklist

## Target Asset

- Filename: `public/images/social/portfolio-og.png`
- Recommended dimensions: `1200 x 630`
- Format: PNG
- Metadata alt text: `Notebook-style portfolio preview for Shivay Dwivedi, Backend Engineer`

Do not configure this image in metadata until the file exists and Shivay approves it for public use.

## Safe Layout

- Keep critical text inside a centered safe area of roughly `1080 x 520`.
- Leave at least `60px` padding on every side.
- Avoid tiny UI text, dense screenshots, or narrow code snippets that will collapse in social cards.
- Keep the notebook identity visible through paper texture, ruled/grid notes, tabs, annotations, or project-evidence cues.

## Suggested Content

- Title: `Shivay Dwivedi`
- Subtitle: `Backend Engineer`
- Supporting line: `Python, Django, Node.js, APIs, database-backed systems`
- Evidence cue: `Five deployed projects`

Do not add user counts, performance metrics, employers, awards, dates, testimonials, or project screenshots unless they are verified and owner-approved.

## Visual Treatment

- Preserve the engineering-notebook identity.
- Use a clean notebook page or desk composition rather than a generic dark developer-card layout.
- Include subtle project/evidence cues, such as small labels for Blogify, Reduc.to, PagePulse, ChatWat, and Sonica.
- Keep contrast high enough for LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Discord, Slack, and messaging previews.

## Preview Checks

- LinkedIn post inspector: confirm title, description, image crop, and no stale cache.
- X/Twitter card validator or live draft preview: confirm `summary_large_image` behavior after image is configured.
- Slack/Discord/iMessage preview: confirm text is legible at small card sizes.
- Browser source check: confirm the Open Graph image URL points to the production domain after final domain verification.
- Cache refresh: trigger platform re-scrapes after each final image change.
